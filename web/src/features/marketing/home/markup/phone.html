What does Spindle do?

Spindle is a GPU compute network design built on Robinhood Chain. Providers register GPU nodes and serve AI jobs in the open lane; sealed is in testing. Each job emits a signed receipt and verified earnings accrue in USDG. The payout design offers USDG, canonical Stock Tokens or a virtual card, with split claims supported. This site presents the v1.1 specification and an illustrative dashboard; it does not display live earnings or guarantee demand.
